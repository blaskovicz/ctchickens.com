WELCOME TO YOUR CT CHICKENS PHOTO FOLDER!
=========================================

This specific Google Drive folder is connected directly to your listing on ctchickens.com. 
You can use this folder to manage the photos displayed on your profile without needing to log in to a website.

HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Upload photos here.
2. Wait for the hourly system scan.
3. Your photos appear live on the website.


INSTRUCTIONS
------------

1. SETTING YOUR LOGO
   To update your main profile picture (logo), upload an image that has the word "logo" in the filename.
   
   Examples that work:
   - logo.png
   - MyFarm-Logo.jpg
   - logo_final.jpeg

   (Note: If you have multiple files with "logo" in the name, the system might pick one at random, so please only keep one logo file in here at a time.)


2. ADDING GALLERY PHOTOS
   Any other image you upload to this folder will automatically be added to your "Gallery" section. 
   These are great for showing off your birds, your coop, or your available products.


IMPORTANT RULES
---------------
* SIZE LIMIT: Please keep individual photos under 10MB. 
    The system will automatically delete files larger than 10MB to prevent storage issues.

* FILE TYPES: Only image files (JPG, PNG, JPEG) are supported. 
    Word docs, PDFs, or videos will be ignored.

* PRIVACY: Do not change the "Share" settings of this folder. 
    Our system automatically handles the permissions to make the images viewable on the web.


TIMING
------
Our system "Janitor" scans this folder once every hour. 
If you upload photos now, please allow up to 60 minutes for them to appear on the website.
